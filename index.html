Passive-Legged Hopping Quadcopter

Graduate research · Microrobotics Lab, CMU · Jan 2026 to present

A 35 g quadcopter with spring-like passive legs that both hops and flies. I built a hybrid stance-flight dynamics model and a predictive controller from empirically identified return maps that holds continuous hopping to 0.1 m RMSE while tracking a reference trajectory. I am now training an off-policy RL agent to capture the unmodeled dynamics and extend hopping to longer, more energy efficient paths.

Airflow Sensing with a Flow Whisker

Graduate research · Microrobotics Lab, CMU · 2026 to present

A neural network that reads 3D relative airflow velocity from a bio-inspired magnetic whisker sensor, mounted on a Crazyflie. It takes the whisker signal and the drone's onboard flight EKF estimates as inputs and maps them to ground-truth airflow, decoupling ambient wind from the drone's own motion for outdoor wind estimation. Trained indoors with easy data acquisition, to be used outdoors. Trained flying under motion capture in a still room for the true relative airflow, and a strong blower of known output velocity.

learned mappingwhisker sensorflight EKFCrazyflie

Gait Control for an Encoderless Quadruped

Graduate research · Microrobotics Lab, CMU · Jan 2026 to present

Gait control for an 8 cm quadruped whose legs carry no encoders. The only signal is each motor's current draw, so I trained a current-sensing MLP classifier that reads stance versus swing per leg at 0.93+ balanced accuracy. A phase-based controller then regulates stride frequency and touchdown timing with PD control, validated in MuJoCo and on hardware.

MLP classifierstate estimationphase-based gaitembedded

Rotor and Swash Plate Dynamics

B.Tech Thesis · IIT Madras · Nov 2024 to Mar 2025

A high fidelity ADAMS/View multibody model of helicopter blade and swash plate dynamics that captures collective and cyclic pitch control and blade-flap response under varying aerodynamic loads. I validated it analytically in MATLAB, matching blade-flap response across a range of lift and drag loads.
